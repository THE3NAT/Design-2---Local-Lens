Logo Design

The Localens logo is a minimalist wordmark designed to reflect the brand's focus on capturing authentic, local perspectives. The two L characters mimic the hand gesture used to frame a photo, while the O represents the subject within the frame—visually reinforcing the act of storytelling through a lens.
The logo uses Noto Sans for its clean, modern, and globally accessible style. Its geometric yet friendly design supports clarity and readability across cultures, aligning with Localens' international audience and inclusive mission.
The playful framing concept paired with a clean typeface makes the logo feel creative yet trustworthy—appealing to travelers, creators, and locals alike.
The design is simple and scalable, maintaining clarity at all sizes. A clearspace equal to the height of the "O" should be kept around the logo to ensure visual impact and avoid clutter.
