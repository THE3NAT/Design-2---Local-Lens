Typography

When we we're picking fonts we had 2 goals in mind. It needed to be clean. The general look and theme of our app is very professional, so we needed a font that would fit that theme. This in addition to the inherent 'digital-ness' of the project basically ruled out any serif fonts. The font needed to be versatile. We want a clean look, and one of the ways we're going about that is keeping to a single font family for the entire app. We needed something with lots of styles so that we wouldn't have to compromise our overall design based on font limitations. At that point we had narrowed it down to Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, and Open Sans. We did a quick vote and Open Sans won.
