Typography

When we we're picking fonts we had 2 goals in mind. It needed to be clean. The general look and theme of our app is very professional, so we needed a font that would fit that theme. This in addition to the inherent 'digital-ness' of the project basically ruled out any serif fonts. The font needed to be versatile. We want a clean look, and one of the ways we're going about that is keeping to a single font family for the entire app. We needed something with lots of styles so that we wouldn't have to compromise our overall design based on font limitations. At that point we had narrowed it down to Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, and Open Sans. We did a quick vote and Open Sans won. All of our fonts use our "Almost White" and "Is That Black" colour styles #F1F1F1 and #E0E7FF. We also support multiple languages! ¡También admitimos varios idiomas!

Inclusivity

Here at LocaLens, we believe in the expression of the self. We want all of our users to use our platform as a place where they can show who they truly are through their photographs. Upon sign up, users will have the option of displaying their personal pronouns on their profile page. This is accomplished not by a set of choices, but through the user's own input because only oneself can speak for who they are. Pronouns are personal, and can mean different things for each individual and everything regarding who you are as a person is respected and entirely optional.
