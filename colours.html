Colours

We attribute our app's signature colour ("Gun Orange"—cleverly named as a nod to the "shooting" aspect of photography) to the one commonality that binds all photos—light.

The Localens palette blends clean neutrals with vibrant accents to reflect the brand's focus on creativity, exploration, and cultural connection.
"That's White", "Almost White", "Yes! Black", "Is That Black?" provide a modern, minimal base ideal for clarity, structure, and contrast.
"Grapes?" and "Gun Orange" are the accent colours—"Grapes?" reflects creativity and openness, while "Gun Orange" adds warmth and energy, evoking a sense of adventure.
"Go Go Go" represents growth and success, while "Warm Red" highlights urgency or key actions and "Pookie Yellow" indicates caution.

This palette supports a vibrant yet clean visual identity that's versatile and globally appealing.
